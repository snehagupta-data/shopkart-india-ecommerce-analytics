# Business Findings

## Revenue & Growth
ShopKart generated approximately ₹1.75Bn in revenue with 43.90% YoY growth across approximately 150K orders.

## Profitability
Profit is approximately ₹215.30M at a 12.33% margin. Low-margin revenue represents 38.80% of revenue, while 8.56% is loss-making.

## Discounting
₹197M of discounts were applied, and 80.23% of revenue came from discounted sales. Higher discount bands show significant margin pressure, especially in Electronics.

## Customers
Repeat customers represent approximately 67.53% of customers. 4,682 customers are classified as At Risk, representing approximately ₹219.89M in revenue exposure.

## Returns
Direct return impact is approximately ₹153.20M, combining refunds and return costs. Size/Fit and Product Quality are among the leading return reasons.

## Operations
On-time delivery is approximately 82.74%, with around 26K delayed shipments and approximately 1K severe delays.

## Business Direction
The analysis points toward five major areas of attention: discount discipline, product-level profitability, customer retention, return reduction and fulfillment performance.
